Designer and Developer

Sample Student Thinking Generator

Designer and Developer

A free AI tool that generates realistic sample student responses to a teacher's own question, spanning grade-appropriate correctness and well-documented misconceptions, each with an asset-based read of what the response demonstrates. Built for "My Favorite No" style discussion prep.

Teacher Deck AI Skill

Designer and Developer

A free, open AI Skill that turns a found lesson plan into a slide deck matching a school's own template, with openly licensed images and teacher notes. Every slide comes with teacher notes, timings, and scaffolds for emerging students. Part of a "Brief, Build, Check" pattern for classroom AI work that keeps the teacher in charge of judgment.

Smithsonian Science for Computational Thinking & Makerspaces

Lead Digital Producer

At the Smithsonian Science Education Center I helped build the digital experiences behind two free curriculum lines. Smithsonian Science for Computational Thinking is a set of instructional units that integrate STEM and computational thinking, where elementary students work through real-world problems and scientific phenomena. Smithsonian Science for Makerspaces is a series of engineering design challenges with 3-D models and lesson plans that walk students through observing, making, designing, and testing, aligned to the Next Generation Science Standards and developed with support from Johnson & Johnson. Both libraries are free for any classroom.
